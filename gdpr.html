GDPR Compliance

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

EU, EEA, and Switzerland: This page explains the GDPR rights that apply if you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area, or Switzerland. It is not a GDPR certification. Security claims on this page follow the Security page, which is limited to practices verified on 17 August 2026.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your account and personal data
  • Restrict processing while a request is being checked
  • Port data you provided, in a structured format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing
  • Complain to a supervisory authority

The categories we actually hold are described in the Privacy Policy: name and email, optional profile details, cruise and port information you add, usage and device data, location when you allow it, and photos or other content you upload.

How to exercise your rights

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Privacy & Data to request a copy of your data, or Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account to delete it. Those steps are also written up on Manage your app data and Delete your account.

You can also email privacy@seadays.app. Data requests are free.

  • We respond within 30 days of receiving the request.
  • Complex requests may take up to 60 days. We will tell you if we need that extra time.

Request my data · Delete my data

Legal basis

  • Contract: to provide the SeaDays app and the features in our Terms
  • Consent: location, marketing messages, and non-essential website cookies and ads, which you can refuse
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure, understanding how it is used, and answering support requests
  • Legal obligation: when the law requires us to keep or disclose information

Processors

App and account services:

  • Supabase: Database, authentication, and file storage
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and in-app purchase management
  • Google AdMob: Advertising in the mobile app for free-tier users
  • Google Firebase Analytics: In-app analytics
  • Sentry: Error tracking and crash reporting
  • Sign in with Apple: Account sign-in

Website (seadays.app), which is hosted on GitHub Pages:

  • Google Analytics 4: Website analytics, with Consent Mode v2. Analytics storage stays denied until you accept analytics.
  • Google AdSense: Display ads on selected blog articles, only after you accept advertising cookies

Details of ads, consent, and analytics are in the Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. The website is served over HTTPS, and app APIs use Supabase. Sign-in uses Supabase Auth.

We do not claim encryption at rest, multi-factor authentication, or a third-party penetration test. A technical security audit was completed on 17 August 2026. What that audit did and did not verify is on the Security page.

International transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove personal data tied to your user ID, except limited records required for law, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal claims, and only for as long as those purposes require. See Delete your account.

Children's Privacy

You must be at least 16 to use SeaDays. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

Breach notification

If a personal-data breach requires notice, we notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, where the GDPR requires that notice, and we notify affected people without undue delay when the GDPR requires that too.

Supervisory authority

You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority:

  • EU: European Data Protection Board member list
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office

Changes

We may update this page. We will notify you of significant changes by email or in the app. Continued use of SeaDays after those changes is acceptance of the updated page.
