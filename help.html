Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SeaDays?

SeaDays is a comprehensive cruise planning app that helps you track your voyages, earn XP and achievements, connect with fellow cruisers, and manage every aspect of your maritime adventures.

How do I add a cruise?

You can add cruises through the "Plan" tab in the app. Enter your booking details or connect directly with supported cruise lines to import your itinerary automatically.

What are XP and Achievements?

XP (Experience Points) power your maritime ranks and levels. Live XP comes from logged cruises, posts, photos, ports, days at sea, your SeaDays Points balance (1 XP per point), successful referrals (+500 XP), and achievement bonuses. SeaDays Points are a separate currency used in the Points Shop. Environmental contributions spend Points on an estimated footprint pledge — they are not verified carbon credits and do not award bonus XP or double Points.

Is SeaDays free?

Yes! SeaDays offers a free tier with core features. Premium tiers unlock unlimited cruises, advanced statistics, and exclusive benefits.

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@seadays.app for any questions, bug reports, or feature requests. We typically respond within 24-48 hours.

Can I export my data?

Yes! You can export all your cruise data, statistics, and photos from your account settings at any time.

Offline checklist: what should I do before boarding?

If you expect slow or no internet onboard, download your offline data before you travel. In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Offline & Performance, enable Smart App Cache, then tap Download Offline Data. Right before leaving, tap Update Cached Data once more. This preloads Portside Stories (blogs + images) and key summaries like Cruise Passport and Voyage Analytics for offline viewing.
