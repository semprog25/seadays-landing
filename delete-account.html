Delete your SeaDays account

Last updated: September 19, 2026

SeaDays lets you permanently delete your account and the personal data tied to it. This page explains how to do that in the mobile app and how to reach us if you cannot sign in.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. You will not be able to recover your profile, cruises, points, achievements, notes, or other app data after deletion completes.

Delete in the SeaDays app (recommended)

You must be signed in. For your security, SeaDays may ask you to verify your identity again before deletion.

  1. Open SeaDays and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon).
  3. Open the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read the warnings, then confirm on the final screen with Delete Permanently.

When deletion succeeds, you are signed out and your account is removed from our systems.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes data associated with your user ID, including for example:

  • Profile, username, and preferences
  • Cruise plans, bookings you saved, packing lists, notes, and expenses
  • Points, XP, achievements, and referral data linked to your account
  • Your Supabase authentication account and related database records that are tied to your user ID with delete-on-account rules

Community content you posted may be removed or anonymized as part of account cleanup, depending on how it is stored.

Data we may retain

We may keep limited information only where required by law or for legitimate business needs, such as:

  • Records needed for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal claims
  • Server or security logs retained for a limited period for operations and abuse prevention

When retention applies, data is kept no longer than necessary for those purposes. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

Cannot access the app?

Email us from the address on your account (or explain why you cannot) and we will verify ownership before processing a deletion request.

Email privacy@seadays.app Email gdpr@seadays.app

We aim to respond within 30 days (up to 60 days for complex requests, with notice).

Manage data without deleting your account

If you only want a copy of your data or to change specific information, see Manage your app data.
