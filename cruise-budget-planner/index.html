Frequently asked questions

What cruise costs should I budget for?

Include fare, taxes, gratuities, excursions, specialty dining, drinks, Wi‑Fi, spa, and souvenirs. SeaDays helps you capture categories most spreadsheets miss.

Can SeaDays help with drink package math?

Yes. Use the drink package calculator landing page and Voyage Analytics together to decide whether a package beats pay-as-you-go for your habits.

Is this a bank or accounting app?

No. SeaDays is a cruise vacation planner for passengers. Budget tools are designed for trip planning, not corporate accounting.
