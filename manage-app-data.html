Manage your SeaDays app data

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Use the options below to access, correct, or limit how SeaDays uses your data. To remove your entire account, see Delete your SeaDays account.

In the SeaDays app

Open Profile → Settings → Privacy & Data.

Request a copy of your data

Tap Request Data Download, enter your email, and submit. We use this to verify the request and send you an export of data associated with your account (for example profile information, cruises, and activity history). We aim to respond within 48 hours where possible.

Update profile and content

Edit your profile, cruises, notes, and other content you created directly in the app under Profile and the relevant tabs. This is the fastest way to correct inaccurate information.

Reported & blocked users

Under Privacy & Data, open Reported & blocked to review users you blocked or reported.

Ad privacy (Android & iOS)

On the mobile app, tap Ad privacy options under Privacy & Data to manage personalized advertising consent where supported by your device and our ad partners.

Privacy policy in the app

Open Privacy policy in Settings for the in-app summary, or read the full policy at seadays.app/privacy.html.

Request deletion of specific data (without deleting your account)

SeaDays does not currently offer a separate in-app control to delete individual data categories while keeping your account. If you need certain data removed but want to keep your account, contact us with details. We will verify your identity and process valid requests under applicable privacy law.

privacy@seadays.app gdpr@seadays.app

Typical response time: within 30 days (up to 60 days for complex requests).

EU / UK privacy rights

For access, portability, restriction, and objection rights, see our GDPR page.
