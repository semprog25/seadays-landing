Community Guidelines

🤝 Be Respectful

Treat all community members with kindness and respect. We're all here to share our love of cruising.

🌊 Share Authentically

Share genuine experiences and honest recommendations. Your insights help fellow cruisers make better decisions.

🚫 No Spam or Self-Promotion

Keep content relevant to cruising. Commercial promotions and spam are not allowed without prior approval.

🔒 Respect Privacy

Don't share personal information of others without permission. What happens in the crew stays in the crew.

⚠️ Report Issues

If you see inappropriate content or behavior, report it to our moderation team. Help us keep SeaDays safe and welcoming.

✨ Have Fun!

Share your excitement, celebrate achievements, and enjoy connecting with fellow cruise enthusiasts around the world!
