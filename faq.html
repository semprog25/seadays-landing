Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SeaDays

Q What is SeaDays?
SeaDays is a comprehensive cruise planning app that helps you organize your cruise vacations, track your maritime adventures, earn XP and achievements, connect with fellow cruisers, and share your experiences with a vibrant community.
Q How does the XP and leveling system work?
Earn XP by logging cruises, posts, photos, ports, and days at sea. Your SeaDays Points balance also contributes 1 XP per point. Successful referrals award +500 XP to both people. Progress through 30 nautical ranks. Environmental contributions spend SeaDays Points only — they do not award bonus XP or double Points, and they are pledged contributions on an estimated footprint (not verified carbon credits). Pledges can use any SeaDays Points, including Points bought in the app. A pledge does not currently fund any project or reduce emissions.
Q Is SeaDays free to use?
Yes. SeaDays is free to use, including roll calls, with optional paid upgrades. XP is earned from live activity and referrals — there is no live subscription XP multiplier.
Q What are the different subscription tiers?
SeaDays has a free tier and optional paid upgrades (Basic, Pro, and Premium). Prices are shown in the App Store or Google Play before you buy and may vary by country, currency and tax. Paid upgrades can include more saved trips, ad-free browsing, and additional planning tools. Roll calls are free in SeaDays. XP is earned from live activity and referrals — there is no live subscription XP multiplier.
Q Can I use SeaDays with my crew/travel group?
Absolutely! SeaDays includes crew collaboration features. Create a crew, invite members, share itineraries, chat in real-time, and plan your cruise adventure together. Perfect for families, friends, or solo cruisers looking to meet new people!
Q How do I track my cruise history?
Simply add your past and upcoming cruises to your profile. SeaDays automatically tracks ports visited, days at sea, miles sailed, and creates beautiful visualizations of your maritime journey on interactive maps.
Q What kind of achievements can I unlock?
Unlock badges for visiting specific ports, completing certain numbers of cruises, reaching XP milestones, social engagement, and special events. Each badge adds to your maritime credentials and shows off your cruising expertise!
Q Is my data secure?
Yes! We use industry-standard encryption, secure authentication, and follow strict data protection practices. We never sell your personal data. Check our Privacy Policy for complete details.
Q Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID subscriptions (iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). You keep paid features until the end of the billing period.
Q Is SeaDays available on iOS and Android?
Yes. SeaDays is available as a native iOS app on the App Store and a native Android app on Google Play. The marketing site at seadays.app also works in mobile browsers.
Q How do I contact support?
Email support@seadays.app and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
Q Can I use SeaDays offline?
Yes — for offline-ready sections you’ve downloaded ahead of time. Before you travel (or board), open the app and go to Profile → Settings → Offline & Performance. Enable Smart App Cache, tap Download Offline Data, then tap Update Cached Data right before leaving. This helps keep Portside Stories (blogs + images) and key summaries like Cruise Passport and Voyage Analytics available even with weak internet.
Q What is the Points Shop?
Earn SeaDays Points through live in-app actions (posts, cruises, reviews, referrals, and more). Spend them in the Points Shop on catalogue rewards such as Ad-Free Day/Week, draft slots, and other unlocks. Points pledges toward an estimated footprint. These are not carbon offsets or verified credits. Pledges can use any SeaDays Points, including Points bought in the app. A pledge does not currently fund any project or reduce emissions. Points spending does not award a double Points rebate.
Q How does the footprint pledge work?
Points pledges toward an estimated footprint. These are not carbon offsets or verified credits. SeaDays estimates your cruise CO₂ footprint from voyage details such as distance, ports, and duration. You may spend SeaDays Points (200 or 500 points per ton, or a custom amount at/above the minimum) to record that pledge. It does not retire credits and does not mean trees have been planted. There is no double Points or bonus XP reward for these contributions. Pledges can use any SeaDays Points, including Points bought in the app. A pledge does not currently fund any project or reduce emissions.
Q Are roll calls free?
Yes. Joining, reading and posting in your sailing's roll call is free for everyone.
