About SeaDays

The Ultimate Cruise Planning Companion

Our Mission

Cruise guides & blog

SeaDays publishes independent cruise tips, ship guides, and port planning articles at seadays.app/blog. Articles are written and maintained by the SeaDays team unless a byline names an individual contributor.

Our Mission

SeaDays was created to revolutionize how cruisers plan, track, and share their nautical adventures. We believe that cruise planning should be exciting, social, and rewarding – not stressful.

What We Do

We provide a comprehensive platform that combines:

🎮 Gamification - Earn XP, unlock achievements, and level up as you cruise
🚢 Cruise Tracking - Organize all your past, present, and future voyages
👥 Social Features - Connect with fellow sailors and build your crew
📊 Detailed Statistics - Track your maritime milestones and memories
💾 Offline & Performance - Download offline data and use smart caching before you sail

Why SeaDays?

Traditional cruise planning tools are fragmented and boring. We've built SeaDays to be the one app that does it all – with a beautiful interface, powerful features, and a vibrant community of cruise enthusiasts.
