What this number represents

This is the growing total of CO₂e contributions recorded by the SeaDays community against estimated cruise footprints.

Every contribution starts with a voyage: its distance, duration and port calls help us estimate the journey’s footprint. Cruisers can then use SeaDays Points to make a contribution against that estimate.

How we estimate your voyage footprint

SeaDays uses three parts of your voyage to create an estimate: distance travelled, time at sea and port calls.

How far the voyage travels.

Methodology

Assumptions used in the estimate:

  • ~0.4 kg CO₂e per passenger per nautical mile
  • ~0.15 tonnes per day for time at sea
  • ~0.05 tonnes per port call
  • Weighted together as 60% distance, 30% time at sea, 10% ports
  • A small minimum so short or incomplete itineraries still produce a usable figure

Turning voyages into positive action

Cruising creates memories, connects people and moves us around the world. We want SeaDays to help give something back.

We’re currently working on the right environmental initiative to support with our growing community contribution. When that partnership is in place, we’ll share it openly here and across our social channels.

Where we’re heading

SeaDays is building a community contribution that can support meaningful environmental action.

As the community grows, we’re working toward choosing an environmental initiative that aligns with the values behind SeaDays: protecting the places we travel to and helping make future journeys more responsible.

We’ll announce the initiative publicly once it is confirmed.
