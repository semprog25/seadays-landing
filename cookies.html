Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. SeaDays uses cookies in the mobile app and on the website (seadays.app) for the purposes described below.

Website Analytics Cookies (seadays.app)

On our website we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use pages such as the homepage, blog, ship and port guides, and feature pages. We use Google Consent Mode v2 with analytics and advertising storage denied by default.

If you choose Accept analytics or Accept analytics & ads, we set analytics_storage to granted so GA4 can use analytics cookies. Choosing analytics alone does not grant advertising consent. If you choose Decline all, analytics storage stays denied.

This control affects seadays.app analytics and advertising cookie preferences. Mobile app ad consent is managed in the SeaDays app under Profile → Settings → Ad privacy options.

Website Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

On selected blog article pages we may use Google AdSense to show display ads. AdSense may set cookies or use similar storage for advertising, frequency capping, and measurement. Personalized advertising is requested only when you choose Accept analytics & ads, which grants Consent Mode signals including ad_storage, ad_user_data, and ad_personalization.

If you do not grant advertising consent, we do not load AdSense ad requests for personalized website advertising. We do not enable Google Auto Ads on seadays.app.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Required for app functionality, authentication, and security Session / 1 year
Performance Cookies Help us understand how users interact with SeaDays 1 year
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and customizations (including your website analytics/advertising choices stored locally) 1 year
Analytics Cookies Website: GA4 measurement when you accept analytics. App: usage analytics to improve services Up to 2 years (provider-dependent)
Advertising Cookies Website: Google AdSense on selected blog articles when you accept advertising. App: AdMob for free-tier users subject to in-app consent Provider-dependent

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account (app)
  • Remember your preferences (theme, language, settings)
  • Understand how you use SeaDays to improve features
  • Measure website page views and key actions on seadays.app when you consent to analytics
  • Show and measure Google AdSense ads on selected blog articles when you consent to advertising
  • Analyze app performance and fix issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

Managing Cookies

You have control over cookies through:

  • Website (seadays.app): Use the cookie banner, or the button above, to Decline all, Accept analytics, or Accept analytics & ads
  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies
  • Mobile Settings: iOS and Android have privacy settings for app tracking
  • App Preferences: Adjust ad privacy options in SeaDays settings

Note: Disabling essential cookies may affect app functionality. Declining website analytics or advertising does not block browsing seadays.app.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies:

  • Authentication providers (Supabase)
  • Google Analytics 4 on the website (when you accept analytics)
  • Google AdSense on selected blog articles (when you accept advertising)
  • Analytics and advertising services in the mobile app
  • Payment processors
  • Map and location services

These third parties have their own cookie policies that we recommend reviewing.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
