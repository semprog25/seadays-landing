Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. SeaDays uses cookies in the mobile app and on the website (seadays.app) for the purposes described below.

Website Analytics Cookies (seadays.app)

On our website we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use pages such as the homepage, blog, ship and port guides, and feature pages. We use Google Consent Mode v2 with analytics and advertising storage denied by default.

If you choose Accept on the cookie banner, we set analytics_storage to granted so GA4 can use analytics cookies. Advertising-related consent signals (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) remain denied for this website analytics setup. If you choose Decline, analytics storage stays denied.

This control only affects seadays.app analytics cookies. Mobile app ad consent is managed in the SeaDays app under Profile → Settings → Ad privacy options.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Required for app functionality, authentication, and security Session / 1 year
Performance Cookies Help us understand how users interact with SeaDays 1 year
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and customizations (including your website analytics Accept/Decline choice stored locally) 1 year
Analytics Cookies Website: GA4 measurement when you Accept. App: usage analytics to improve services Up to 2 years (provider-dependent)

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account (app)
  • Remember your preferences (theme, language, settings)
  • Understand how you use SeaDays to improve features
  • Measure website page views and key actions on seadays.app when you consent
  • Analyze app performance and fix issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

Managing Cookies

You have control over cookies through:

  • Website (seadays.app): Use the cookie banner, or the button above, to Accept or Decline analytics cookies
  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies
  • Mobile Settings: iOS and Android have privacy settings for app tracking
  • App Preferences: Adjust ad privacy options in SeaDays settings

Note: Disabling essential cookies may affect app functionality. Declining website analytics does not block browsing seadays.app.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies:

  • Authentication providers (Supabase)
  • Google Analytics 4 on the website (when you Accept)
  • Analytics and advertising services in the mobile app
  • Payment processors
  • Map and location services

These third parties have their own cookie policies that we recommend reviewing.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
