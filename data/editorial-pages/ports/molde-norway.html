Ships in Molde tie up on the town waterfront, so you walk straight off the gangway into town. The reason to come is the view from Varden, the hill above town, where you can see 222 mountain peaks on a clear day.

Molde is a small town on the Romsdal Fjord. Much of the centre burned after German bombing in the spring of 1940 and was rebuilt after the war, so the town looks modern. The mountains across the water are the real draw. On a long call you can also reach two of Norway's best-known drives, the Atlantic Road and Trollstigen.

Things to do

If you have 3 to 4 hours: Varden

Varden viewpoint is 407 metres above sea level, and Visit Norway says you can see 222 peaks from the top, most of them over 1,000 metres. The cruise shuttle run by West Norway Travel takes about 15 minutes from the harbour and gives you about 30 minutes at the top. You can ride back down, or walk the 4.5 km trail into town, which takes about an hour. The Vardestua restaurant at the top is open roughly from May to October. If the peaks are clear when you dock, go up first, because cloud can come in quickly on the fjord.

Back in town, Molde Cathedral is worth a short stop. It was consecrated in 1957 and replaced the wooden church that burned in the 1940 bombing. Molde's old nickname is the Town of Roses.

If you have 5 hours: add Romsdalsmuseet

Romsdalsmuseet is an open-air museum with about 50 buildings from the Romsdal region, the oldest from the 1600s. The museum says its buildings are open with guides on most cruise days in season. An adult ticket was NOK 180 when we checked in October 2026. The address is Per Amdams veg 4, and the museum lists its cruise opening days on its website.

Top things to do

  • Ride or hike up to Varden for the 222-peak view
  • See Molde Cathedral (1957) in the rebuilt centre
  • Walk through the old houses at Romsdalsmuseet
  • On a full day, take a tour to the Atlantic Road or Trollstigen

Where ships dock

The port authority, Molde og Romsdal Havn, lists Storkaia as Molde's main town quay. It is 300 metres long with 9.5 metres of water alongside. West Norway Travel says Molde has two cruise berths and that its Varden shuttle leaves from the harbour. Check which berth your ship is using before you look for the shuttle.

Varden is a steep climb above town, so take the shuttle or a taxi up if you are short on time or not up for a hill walk. Agree on the taxi fare before you set off.

If you have a full day: the Atlantic Road or Trollstigen

These two drives go in different directions from Molde, so pick one. A guided tour or the ship's own excursion takes the worry about all-aboard time off your hands.

The Atlantic Road

The Atlantic Road links the island of Averøy with the mainland across a string of low islands. Visit Norway gives its length as 8,274 metres, with eight bridges, and says it opened in 1989. Norwegian Scenic Routes counts seven bridges and treats the whole 36 km stretch from Kårvåg to Bud as the scenic route. At Eldhusøya there is a raised walkway along the shore and a rest area.

Trollstigen

Trollstigen is the hairpin mountain road above Åndalsnes. It closes every winter and normally opens from mid-May until October or November. In 2026 it opened on 27 April. From 28 September 2026 the road was closed on weekdays from 07:00 to 18:00 for up to four weeks of rockfall work. Check the current status with the Norwegian Public Roads Administration before you book a late-season tour.

Before you go ashore

  • Check which of the two cruise berths your ship uses, so you know where the Varden shuttle picks up.
  • Pack a rain layer, even in summer. The view from Varden depends on the weather.
  • Planning Trollstigen late in the season? Check the road status the day before your call.
  • Romsdalsmuseet opens for ship calls, so check its cruise-day list before you walk up.

Traveler questions

How do I get from the ship to Varden?

The cruise shuttle from the harbour takes about 15 minutes and gives you about 30 minutes at the top. You can also walk the 4.5 km trail, which takes about an hour each way, or take a taxi.

Can I see the Atlantic Road on a Molde port call?

Yes, on a long call with a tour or ship excursion. The road is a separate trip from Trollstigen, so choose one of the two.
