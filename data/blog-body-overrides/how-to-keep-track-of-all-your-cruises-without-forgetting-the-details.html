What is worth writing down

After a cruise, the details that are hard to reconstruct are the ones that were never in one place: the ship, the dates, the cabin number, who you traveled with, and what you actually paid. A photo of the folio and a line in a note are enough. You do not need a scoring system.

Record the sailing the line sold you (ship, embarkation port, dates), the cabin category you booked versus the cabin you got, and the ports where you had to tender. If you want a cost memory, copy the folio total and the categories that surprised you. Those are your numbers. A blog cannot supply them.

SeaDays can hold that note with the sailing. A spreadsheet does the same job. The failure mode is leaving it in a chat that nobody can search next year.
