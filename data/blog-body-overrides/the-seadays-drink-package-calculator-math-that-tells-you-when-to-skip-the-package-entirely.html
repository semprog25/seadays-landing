The ninety-second decision at the embarkation desk

You are standing at Deck 4 on boarding day. A host offers the unlimited beverage package at "today's onboard price." The line behind you is moving, the math is opaque, and the number is bigger than you expect. Most cruisers make that call on instinct, then spend the rest of the week either overpaying for drinks they did not consume or under-ordering because they know every cocktail is already adding up.

If you have not yet clarified what your line includes, cabin rules, and gratuity treatment, start with the real cost of cruise drink packages—this page is the arithmetic once you know what you are comparing.

The SeaDays Drink Package Calculator exists because that guess is almost always wrong in one direction, and the direction depends on which line you are sailing, the region, and what you actually drink. A Caribbean Royal Caribbean sailing and a Mediterranean Virgin Voyages sailing do not follow the same pricing rules, so a blanket "always buy the package" or "never buy the package" rule costs money either way.

What the calculator actually does

The tool lives in Discover and takes four inputs: region, cruise line, cruise length in days, and your honest daily drink count by type — beer, wine, cocktails/martinis, and specialty coffee. It outputs three numbers: the total package cost with gratuity baked in where the line publishes it that way, the total à la carte cost at your drink mix, and the break-even delta. If the delta is negative, the package loses money for you. If it is positive, you save.

Three things make the output trustworthy. First, pricing is per cruise line and per region, so Royal Caribbean's Caribbean package rate is not mixed with its Mediterranean rate. Second, currency toggles between USD, EUR, and GBP so you are comparing in the currency your shipboard folio will post in. Third, coffee is a first-class input. Specialty coffee is easy to leave out of the count. Use the price on the menu, not a weekly total from this page.

Inputs you need before you tap calculate

  • Package price from your invoice or pre-cruise offer, per person, for the full sailing—including any service charge the line lists separately if your comparison requires it.
  • Menu prices for the drinks you actually order from the covered list, not aspirational cocktails you might try once.
  • Days at sea vs port days if your pattern differs; you can enter a blended daily count or run two scenarios.
  • Inclusions for your line: whether coffee, bottled water, or premium tiers are inside the package you selected.

Step-by-step: running the calculator before you sail

Open Discover and tap Drink Package Calculator. Select your region (Caribbean, Mediterranean, Northern Europe, Alaska, Transatlantic, and so on). Only lines that actually sail that region appear, which cuts the noise. Pick your cruise line. Set the number of days. Use the +/– counters (capped at 15 per type so you cannot calculate a fantasy scenario) to enter the drinks you honestly expect each day.

Switch the currency to match your folio. Read the three numbers at the bottom. Screenshot the result or save your voyage context in SeaDays so the calculation sits next to your packing list and port notes. Re-run the calculator 48 hours into the cruise using data from the Drink Tracker when your early habits differ from your guess. Read the cancellation window on the package you are buying—it is not the same 24 or 48 hours on every line.

How to run the comparison manually

Do not use a sample price from a blog. On your invoice, write the package price per person per day, including the gratuity the line adds to it. On the line’s current bar menu, write what you actually drink. Multiply. If the menu total is lower than the package, the package costs more for that pattern. If you will not drink that pattern every day, adjust the daily count downward before you buy.

A worked example with made-up menu prices is how older versions of this page called the difference a loss. Those prices were not a live tariff for any line. Virgin’s bar tab, when the line sells one, follows whatever the current offer says—not Carnival-style unlimited logic.

Honest consumption estimates

Count what you drink on land vacations and sea days separately if your itinerary is port-heavy—see when the beverage package actually loses money for why that matters. Port-day consumption is often lower than sea-day consumption when you are off the ship for six to nine hours.

Specialty coffee is the silent lever. If you drink it daily, add the menu price to your à la carte side. If you do not drink it, leave the counter at zero. Status perks, casino offers, or fare-included drinks should not be double-counted on the pay-as-you-go side.

Use the Drink Tracker for 48 hours of real data, then re-run. Most package cancellations are allowed in the first 24–48 hours of a sailing on many lines—confirm on yours.

Line-specific inclusion differences (verify on your sailing)

MSC Premium Extra bundles often treat specialty coffee and select bottled water differently from a beer-and-wine-only plan. Carnival Cheers! coffee rules have varied by fare and promotion—check your booklet. Celebrity Classic vs Premium wine caps change whether bottle upgrades sit inside the package. Virgin Voyages bar tabs are sized purchases, not “count cocktails until break-even” math copied from another brand.

The calculator reflects the line and region you select; it does not replace reading the inclusion PDF. When inclusions differ, the same drink count produces a different answer.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not skip the region step — a Mediterranean Royal sailing can price differently than a Caribbean one. Do not count drinks you already have for free (status perks, Casino Royale drink offers, fare-inclusive promotions). Do not add gratuity twice when the displayed package price already includes it. Do not trust onboard "today only" urgency; terminal pricing is often the same as pre-cruise pricing. Do not forget specialty coffee when it is not included in your plan tier.

Common questions

Q: Does the calculator include auto-gratuity?

A: It uses the all-in package figures the line publishes for that region where gratuity is bundled into the marketed price. Match that to how you sum à la carte drinks.

Q: Can I cancel the package after boarding if the calculator flips?

A: Many lines allow cancellation within 24–48 hours of embarkation. Confirm with guest services on your specific sailing.

Q: What if my partner drinks but I do not?

A: Packages are usually sold per adult in the cabin on most lines. Run the calculator once for each person—two separate decisions, not one shared one.

Q: Are prices updated?

A: Pricing is refreshed per line and region. Run the calculator closer to sailing for the freshest numbers.

Q: What if I drink mostly bottled water and soft drinks?

A: Many packages bundle non-alcoholic options separately. Consider a soda-only or water-and-coffee bundle instead of the full bar package.
