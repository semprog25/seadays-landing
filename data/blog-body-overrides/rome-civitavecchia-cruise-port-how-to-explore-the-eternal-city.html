Civitavecchia is not Rome. The cruise port is about 70 km north-west of the city, and the Port Mobility information service puts the regional train ride to Roma San Pietro at 45 to 50 minutes. Add the port shuttle, the walk or bus to the station and the wait for a train, and the trip from gangway to St Peter's is a good part of your morning. Port Mobility's own Rome suggestions assume 6 to 8 free hours. With less time ashore, stay in Civitavecchia or take the ship's tour.

Here is the do-it-yourself route, step by step, with the official prices we checked on 18 October 2026.

The short version

  • Free port shuttle from your ship to Largo della Pace
  • Walk 1.5 km to the station, or take the €6 cruise bus
  • Regional train to Roma San Pietro for €4.60 in second class
  • Walk about 1 km to St Peter's Square

One way, that is €4.60 if you walk to the station, or €10.60 with the bus.

Step 1: From the ship to Largo della Pace

A free cruise shuttle links docks 10, 11, 12, 12bis, 13, 13bis and 25 with Largo della Pace, the service centre at the port gate. Port Mobility says the ride takes 10 to 15 minutes and the shuttles are timed around ship arrivals. If your ship uses a different dock, follow the port signs or ask the crew at the gangway.

Step 2: From Largo della Pace to Civitavecchia station

You have two options:

  • Walk. The station is about 1.5 km from Largo della Pace, 10 to 20 minutes on foot. From the Fortezza pedestrian gate it is only about 600 m.
  • Take the cruise bus run by Civitavecchia Servizi Pubblici. It costs €6 each way, you pay on board, and it only runs on cruise days, from about 07:00 to about 19:00. The ride takes about 15 minutes.

Step 3: The train to Rome

Regional trains on the FL5 line run from Civitavecchia to Rome about every 30 minutes, according to Port Mobility. Get off at Roma San Pietro for the Vatican, or stay on for Roma Ostiense or Roma Termini.

The fare comes from the Lazio regional fare table, which sets prices by distance. A second-class ticket for the 71 to 80 km band costs €4.60. Termini is a little farther along the line, so the fare there may be one band higher. Check the price at the ticket machine before you pay.

Faster Intercity trains also run on this line, but they cost more and leave less often. Check times and prices with Trenitalia before you choose one.

Which ticket to buy

  • Paper regional ticket: validate it in the machine at the station before you board. Trenitalia says you must finish the journey within 4 hours of validation.
  • Digital Regional Ticket: bought on the Trenitalia app or website. It is in your name and only valid on the train you choose, so carry ID. You can change the time on the day of travel until the train leaves.
  • BIRG day ticket: one ticket for the day that covers regional trains and Rome's buses, trams and metro. The 5-zone BIRG costs €14 in the 2026 Lazio fare table. Compare it with two regional tickets plus the city rides you plan to take.

Where to get off

  • St Peter's and the Vatican: Roma San Pietro. Port Mobility says the basilica is about 1 km away, 15 to 20 minutes on foot, or you can take bus 64.
  • The Colosseum: Roma Ostiense. Walk to Piramide and take metro line B to Colosseo. Port Mobility puts the whole trip from Civitavecchia station at about 1 hour 10 minutes.
  • The Pantheon and Piazza Navona: Roma San Pietro, then bus 64 to Largo Argentina and a 5-minute walk. Allow about 1 hour 25 minutes from Civitavecchia station.
  • The Spanish Steps and the Trevi Fountain: Roma Termini, then metro line A to Spagna. Allow about 1 hour 30 minutes.

Short on time at the Vatican? Timed-entry tickets help you skip the ticket queue, but security checks still apply.

Other ways to get to Rome

  • SIT bus shuttle: €15 one way or €25 return. Buses leave the port, from the exit of the Welcome Centre at Largo della Pace, at 09:00 and 09:30. They reach the Vatican at about 10:00 or 10:30 and Termini at about 10:15 or 10:45, depending on traffic. This timetable runs from 15 March to 15 November 2026. Check the return times with SIT before you book.
  • Taxi from Rome back to the port: Rome-licensed taxis charge a fixed fare of €150 from inside the Aurelian Walls to the port of Civitavecchia, set by the City of Rome. From Fiumicino airport to the port, the fixed fare is €130.
  • Ship's tour: costs more, but transport is arranged for you around the ship's schedule.

Getting back to the ship

Cruise lines do not have to wait for guests who travel on their own. Plan to be back at Civitavecchia station well before all-aboard. The cruise bus to the port stops running at about 19:00. After that, you walk 1.5 km to Largo della Pace for the port shuttle.

There is no official luggage storage at the port, so this plan only works on a port call, not on the day you disembark with your bags.

Strike days

Italian transport strikes are announced in advance on the strike calendar of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport. During a rail strike, Trenitalia runs a list of guaranteed regional trains on weekdays from 06:00 to 09:00 and from 18:00 to 21:00. If a strike falls on your port day, check the guaranteed trains or book the ship's tour.
